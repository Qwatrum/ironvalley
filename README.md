# Iron Valley

---

###### a mod adding a new biome
---

## Features:
- One new biome
- One new block
- One new item

## New Biome:
The Iron Valley is a new hot biome where large amounts of iron occur. The new block makes most part out of it.
Iron themed, the rivers are redish and the vegetation is small and only some trees grow.
As iron is a pretty good conductor if a lightning bolt hits the new iron dirt block, the nearest player will get an electric shock with its strength depending on his distance.
There is also a really small chance that a skeleton horse will spawn, they can only spawn at the edge of the biome!
There're also many dust particles

## New Block:
The Iron dirt block is a new block. It can't be mined. And it can't be crafted.

## New Item:
The sieve is a special new item, crafted from nine chains the player now has the possibility to get iron nuggets from iron dirt
